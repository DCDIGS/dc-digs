Welcome to DC Digs

A daily inbox of Bandcamp releases picked for your taste. Tell it what you like; it digs every day and you just listen and sort.

1 Reference tracks

Paste links to Bandcamp tracks or albums you love, one per line. DC Digs looks at what the people who bought them also bought, and brings you the releases several of them share, in the same genre.
Example: https://artist.bandcamp.com/track/some-track

5–10 references is a good start. You can change them any time in Settings.

2 A second inbox optional

Dig for a second style side by side, with its own reference tracks. Leave empty if you don't need it.

3 Your Bandcamp name optional

If you follow artists and labels on Bandcamp, their new releases come into the first inbox too. Your name is the part after bandcamp.com/ on your fan page.

4 When to dig

Once a day, while DC Digs is running. If the computer is off then, it catches up the next time it starts.

DC Digs runs only on this computer and talks to Bandcamp the way a browser does. It isn't made or endorsed by Bandcamp.
